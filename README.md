### Hi, I'm Luís Augusto!
I'm a senior-year Computer Science student at **PUC-SP** and the president of **PUCTech**, the university's first academic league dedicated to Science and Technology.

I enjoy teaching, learning, and building academic projects that help other students explore Computer Science beyond the classroom. I believe in using technology to solve problems, share knowledge, and build stronger academic communities.

#### My Interests

I'm particularly interested in Software Engineering and Machine Learning, from designing and building backend systems to exploring how data can be used to solve problems. I'm also interested in software architecture, databases, and the principles behind reliable software.
